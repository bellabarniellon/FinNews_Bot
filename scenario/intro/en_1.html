Scenario analyser: what it is and how to read it Every morning this topic gets a 6-month outlook for the S&P 500, KOSPI, gold, Brent, the US 10-year yield, USD/KZT and USD/KRW. It is not a single-number forecast. The model looks at how markets behaved after similar states in the past and builds 20,000 paths from that. Table • Median in 6 months: half the paths end above, half below. • Range, 5th–95th pct: 90% of paths end inside it. This is the model's main output. • Chance higher: share of paths ending above today's level. • Median change: shift versus the previous run. Charts The black line is the last 6 months, the blue line is the median path, the dark band holds the middle 50% of paths and the light band 90%. The caption flags only what moved the numbers materially that day.
